Recorded on the service label (SANS 1475 clause 6.1). Weigh the extinguisher per the manufacturer's instructions.
CO₂ and gas-cartridge extinguishers are weighed every 6 months (SANS 10105-1). Recharge if the content is below the manufacturer's tolerance.
"Pressure test due" auto-fills to 5 years after the last test for powder, foam and water extinguishers, or 10 years for CO₂. Hydrants, sirens and blankets get no auto-filled date. The last-test month carries over between services — change it only in the year a test is actually done.

Monthly inspection

SANS 10105-1: the responsible person visually checks each unit every month — correct location, unobstructed and visible, safety pin and tamper seal intact, pressure indicator in range, no visible damage or corrosion, service label current. Record the walk-through here; use the printable log for a signed record on site.
